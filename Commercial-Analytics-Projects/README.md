## Project Case Study: Multi-Tier Intelligence Capacity & Utilization Suite

### 🎯 1. Business Problem
* **Productivity Measurement Bias:** Evaluating analysts purely on gross completed ticket volume rewarded trivial, low-effort tasks while penalizing researchers tackling complex multi-entity enterprise cases.
* **Capacity Planning Blind Spots:** Team leads lacked visibility into net productive time versus administrative and training overhead, leading to inaccurate sprint commitments and burnout.
* **TAT SLA Degradation:** Inability to pinpoint system bottlenecks or queue friction resulted in delayed commercial deliverables for regional sales leaders.
* **Incentive Allocation Friction:** Quarterly Incentive Plans (QIP) lacked transparent, auditable mathematical backing, causing team friction and misaligned commercial goals.

---

### 🧠 2. Analytical Approach & Methodology
1. **Normalized Velocity Algorithm:**
   Engineered a weighted scoring equation that equalizes effort across disparate ticket categories:
   $$\text{Normalized Score} = \text{Base Complexity Weight} + (\text{High Effort} \times 2) + \left(\frac{\text{Trivial}}{3}\right) + \left(\frac{\text{Surplus}}{2}\right)$$
2. **Deterministic Capacity Modeling:**
   * Established standard sprint capacity: $480\text{ min/day} \times 0.90\text{ efficiency} = 432\text{ productive min/day}$.
   * Deducted pre-committed hours (QA audits, onboarding, administration) to calculate true net available bandwidth per researcher.
3. **Cohort-Specific Benchmarking:**
   Segmented operational pods into distinct cohorts (Enterprise, Mid-Market, Specialized, Onboarding) with tailored time standards per ticket component (Entity, Domain, Application review).

---

### 💡 3. Solution & Business Impact
* **End-to-End Operational Control Center:** Built a multi-tab business intelligence platform monitoring individual sprint capacity, ticket complexity distributions, system TAT, and QA audit results.
* **Measurable Results:**
  * **60% Reduction** in manual sprint capacity and workload reporting overhead.
  * **40% Acceleration** in deliverable turnaround times (TAT) across high-priority enterprise requests.
  * **100% Data-Driven Governance** for Quarterly Incentive Plans (QIP), ensuring merit-based recognition.
