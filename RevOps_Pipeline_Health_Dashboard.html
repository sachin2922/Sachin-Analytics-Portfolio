Glossary & Definitions

Total Open Deals

Count of all active opportunities that have not been closed won or closed lost.

Health Band

Categorization of deals into Healthy, At Risk, or Critical based on internal engagement and velocity metrics.

Stuck Stage

Deals that have remained in their current stage longer than the historical average for won deals.

Prime vs Non-Prime

Classification of accounts based on strategic importance and ideal customer profile matching.

12M Avg ACV

Trailing 12-month average of Annual Contract Value for all closed won deals.
