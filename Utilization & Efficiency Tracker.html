Intelligence Metric Definitions & Glossary
TermDefinitionFormula / Logic
Full Scope Research TicketA completed account research ticket with priority Critical, Major, or Normal. Covers Account Research and IntelliTrack Account Research record types.Record Type IN {AR, IntelliTrack AR} AND Priority IN {Critical,Major,Normal} AND Status = Completed
Trivial TicketA completed research ticket with Trivial priority — lower effort and weighted less in velocity calculations.Priority = Trivial AND Status = Completed
VettingEnforcement-type ticket where the task is Vetting. Counted separately as it represents a different workflow.Record Type IN {Enforcement, IntelliTrack Enforcement} AND Task = Vetting AND Status = Completed
Normalized VelocityWeighted ticket score that normalises the mix of Enterprise and Mid-Market tickets to create a comparable cross-team performance metric.If Base=High: (Low/2)+High+(Trivials/6)+(Surplus/4) | If Base=Low: Low+(High×2)+(Trivials/3)+(Surplus/2)
Utilization %Percentage of remaining available capacity actually used by a researcher after commitment is deducted.Total Utilization Minutes ÷ Remaining After Commitment Minutes
Capacity Utilization %Ratio of tickets actually completed vs the expected ticket count based on historical average ticket size.Actual Tickets ÷ Expected Tickets (= Remaining Minutes ÷ Benchmark Min/Ticket)
Available Sprint MinutesTotal productive minutes a researcher has in a sprint, accounting for meeting overhead (10% deduction).Working Days × 480 min/day × 0.90 efficiency
Research UtilizationTotal effort minutes logged against full-scope research tickets in the Research Metrics fact table.SUM(Research Metrics[Total_Efforts])
Enforcement EffortsMinutes logged on enforcement-type tickets. Pulled directly from the Ticket Data enforcement time column.SUM(Ticket Data[Enforcement Time])
Trivial EffortsEstimated effort for trivial tickets using a fixed 120-minute benchmark per ticket.Total Trivial Tickets × 120 min
Commitment %Proportion of available sprint minutes pre-committed (QA, training, admin) — capped at 100%. Prorated to date selection.Committed Minutes (Prorated) ÷ Available Sprint Minutes, min(result, 1)
Sprint14-day work cycle. The dashboard covers Sprints 111–163 (FY2025–2026). Each sprint aligns to a 2-week delivery cadence.Sprint 111 = Jan 1 2025 ... Sprint 163 = Dec 31 2026
CohortTeam grouping for Intelligence researchers. Cohorts Branding Team, TeamPro, BrandCo, and Other are excluded from Intelligence metrics.Active cohorts: Cohort A, Cohort B, Cohort C, Cohort D
Benchmark Min/TicketHistorical average effort in minutes per ticket, calculated per cohort type. Used to calculate expected ticket counts.DIVIDE(Remaining After Commitment, Expected Tickets Revamp)
